The horns of war echo once again across the snowy slopes of Alterac. Capture Prison Camps and unleash Cavalry to destroy the enemy General.

Primary Objectives

Invade enemy territory to free allied prisoners from enemy Prison Camps. Escorted prisoners will strengthen your cavalry assault when they reach your base. Once enough prisoners have been freed, the cavalry will charge down all lanes, destroying everything in their path. Slay enemy Heroes to reduce the progress of their cavalry.

First: 1:30 - Respawn: 3:00

Mercenary Camps

Alterac Pass features the following mercenary camps:

  • 2 Siege Giants (top and bottom)
  • 2 Bruiser camps (mid-lane near Prison Camps)
  • 1 Boss (north side, near top Prison Camp)

The Boss camp provides significant pushing power and can help secure Prison Camps.

Tips

  • Coordinate with your team to capture Prison Camps efficiently
  • Escort freed prisoners safely to maximize cavalry strength
  • Contest enemy prisoners to delay their cavalry push
  • Use the Boss to establish map control and secure objectives
